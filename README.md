## Projects

### Proj#1 High-Frequency Trading

This project focuses on challenging traditional financial theories by leveraging high-frequency trading data to uncover potential predictability in price movements. Using a linear regression model, I applied advanced feature engineering techniques to explore subtle but significant patterns within the data. The project demonstrates how predictive models can be employed to develop quantitative trading strategies that may outperform standard market benchmarks. The balance between prediction accuracy and model interpretability is a key focus, ensuring the model's practical applicability in real-world trading environments.

### Proj#2 License Plate Reader
This project focuses on developing an advanced License Plate Recognition (LPR) system using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to address the challenges of traditional LPR methods, such as variable lighting, diverse plate formats, and viewing angles. The system, built on a dataset of Belgian license plates, demonstrates the power of CNNs in recognizing and classifying alphanumeric characters under real-world conditions. By applying techniques like data augmentation, this project explores the potential applications of automated LPR in urban traffic management, law enforcement, and commercial logistics, showcasing how deep learning can enhance accuracy and operational efficiency.

### Proj#3 Airbnb Market Analysis
This project explores the Airbnb market in Los Angeles County through a Power BI dashboard that visualizes key metrics, such as host activity, pricing trends, and geographical distribution of listings. The analysis highlights the popularity of entire home listings, neighborhood price variations, and the correlation between reviews and listing performance. By providing insights into these aspects, the project aims to help hosts and investors optimize their pricing strategies, identify high-performance areas, and improve profitability in the short-term rental market.

### Proj#4 Starbucks Amenity Analysis
This project analyzes the relationship between Starbucks’ amenity offerings—such as Free WiFi, Drive-Thrus, Fizzio Handcrafted Sodas, and Digital Rewards—and the income demographics of surrounding areas in California. By examining store-level data and correlating it with city- and county-level income statistics, the project explores whether Starbucks adjusts its services to match the income levels of its clientele. Key findings reveal strategic variations in amenities like drive-thrus and digital rewards across different income brackets, providing insights into how Starbucks tailors its customer experience based on local demographics.

### Proj#5 HR Analytics
This project features an HR Analytics Dashboard designed in Tableau to provide actionable insights into employee attrition, job satisfaction, and workforce demographics. The dashboard helps HR departments visualize key metrics such as attrition rates, role-based satisfaction levels, and the demographic composition of the workforce. By analyzing these factors, HR professionals can develop targeted retention strategies and make informed decisions to improve overall employee engagement and satisfaction. The project highlights advanced Tableau skills, including calculated fields, filters, and dashboard design, offering a comprehensive tool for data-driven HR management.

### Proj#6 Pizza Sales Analysis
This project explores pizza sales data using PostgreSQL for data querying and Excel for dashboard creation and visualization. The analysis focuses on identifying key sales trends, top-performing products, and customer preferences. By analyzing peak sales periods, product performance, and size preferences, this project provides valuable insights for optimizing business operations and marketing strategies. The use of SQL for data extraction and Excel for visualization highlights proficiency in database management and data analysis. Future improvements include implementing predictive models to forecast sales and performing customer segmentation for targeted marketing.
