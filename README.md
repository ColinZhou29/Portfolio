# Data Analyst Portfolio

## Introduction
Hello!

Welcome to my portfolio!

My name is Colin Zhou, and I am excited to share my journey as a data professional with you. With a background in Business Analytics and Information Systems, I have honed my skills in a wide range of data-centric domains, including data science, machine learning, and statistical analysis. Throughout my academic journey at the University of Southern California and the University of Wisconsin-Madison, I developed a deep proficiency in programming languages like Python and SQL, alongside expertise in data visualization tools such as Power BI and Tableau.

In this repository, you'll find projects that reflect my technical abilities and problem-solving skills, using tools like PostgreSQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI. These projects range from exploratory data analysis to predictive modeling and data-driven decision-making, all aimed at providing valuable insights. Each project demonstrates my ability to not only analyze and process large datasets but also present findings through interactive dashboards and visualizations that facilitate strategic decision-making.

I hope my work inspires meaningful discussions and collaborations, and I look forward to connecting with you on this journey into the world of data analytics!
